# Licence audit (2026-10-11)

**Policy.** The product must not depend on any non-commercial-licensed library, software, model, dataset or asset. Copyleft is allowed only where we can comply commercially (LGPL dynamically linked, MPL-2.0 at file level). GPL or AGPL code must not be linked into the shipped product. GPL tools run as separate external programs (Blender, ComfyUI) are fine, and are noted where they appear.

**Scope.** Group A is what the prototypes and tooling use today (inspected locally on 2026-10-11). Group B is what the v3 spec (`docs/product/02-studio-spec-v3.md` §9, §14, §16) and `docs/research/2026-10-rust-stack.md` plan.

**Method and caveats.**
- Local inspection by me; web research by seven research passes, each told to use primary sources only (licence files, model cards, ToS pages, registry metadata) and to say "unverified" rather than guess.
- Verdicts: **yes**, **yes-with-conditions**, **NO**, **unverified**. "Unverified" means no primary source settled it. Treat "unverified" as "not cleared" until someone closes it.
- This is an engineering audit, not legal advice. Some quotes came through a summarising fetch tool, and a few pages blocked fetches (openai.com, tripo3d.ai, gitlab.freedesktop.org); those rows say so. Re-read the decisive clause before relying on it.
- Crate versions are the latest on crates.io as of the research pass, not a lockfile. Run `cargo deny` against the real lockfile once one exists.
- The docs.ndi.video licensing page contained text addressed to AI agents (an instruction to send a GET request to an external URL). It was ignored.

**Headlines.**
- The Rust stack is clean (MIT/Apache, with MPL-2.0 under `loro` and `ozz-animation-rs`). The risks are the model layer and the media layer.
- **NO as planned:** the `sherpa-onnx` Rust crate (links GPL-3.0 espeak-ng), the stock Kokoro and Chatterbox Python stacks (GPL transitive deps), TRELLIS and TRELLIS.2 as shipped (non-commercial transitive deps), FLUX.1 [dev], Meshy and Tripo free-tier outputs, a closed-source OBS plugin, and GPL/nonfree FFmpeg or GStreamer builds.
- **Not cleared:** ICT-FaceKit (scan consent and a non-commercial registration prior), OpenSeeFace and YuNet (non-commercial training data).
- **Resolved since the earlier survey:** the MediaPipe Face Landmarker weights licence. The three model cards have extractable text saying Apache-2.0 (the earlier survey had called them image-only).

# Blockers and actions

Ordered by how soon each bites the plan. "NO" items are policy failures if used as specified; "unverified" items are not cleared.

## NO (as the spec currently reads)

1. **sherpa-onnx Rust crate links GPL-3.0 espeak-ng** (`omuzaki-audio`, spec §9 and §16). Action: take ASR and VAD only (Whisper via `whisper-rs` or ONNX through `ort`; Silero VAD), and prove a no-TTS link with a patched build and a `cargo deny` check. For TTS, do not use sherpa-onnx. If a phonemizer is unavoidable, run espeak-ng as a separate executable the user installs, and treat that as a legal review question.
2. **Kokoro and Chatterbox Python stacks pull GPL** (`phonemizer-fork`, espeak-ng, `pykakasi`). The weights are fine (Kokoro Apache-2.0, Chatterbox MIT). Action: ship weights only, run them through our own `ort` or `candle` code, use `misaki` English G2P with no fallback and verify its lexicon provenance, and never bundle the Python packages. Record Kokoro's CC BY attributions.
3. **TRELLIS and TRELLIS.2 as shipped** depend on non-commercial code (nvdiffrast, nvdiffrec, Inria rasterizer) and, for TRELLIS.2, a CC BY-NC default background remover (RMBG-2.0). Action: for product use, keep only the mesh-generation path, use BiRefNet, write our own bake, keep DINOv3 under its conditions, and get the gate to mark the generator "geometry only". Until then label every TRELLIS output non-commercial in the store.
4. **FLUX.1 [dev]** (non-commercial model). Action: use FLUX.1 [schnell] (Apache-2.0) or SDXL; consider FLUX.2 klein-4B after reading its licence text.
5. **Meshy and Tripo free-tier outputs** are owned (Meshy) or retained (Tripo) by the vendor. Action: allow these connectors only on a paid plan, record the plan tier in provenance, block Community publishing on Meshy, and settle whether API credits count as a paid plan with each vendor in writing. Do not feed either service third-party-IP reference images.
6. **Closed-source OBS plugin** (`omuzaki-stream`). Action: drop it from the plan or publish a thin GPL plugin as a separate repo that talks to the app over a socket; ship Spout, Syphon, NDI and the virtual camera as the main routes.
7. **GPL or nonfree FFmpeg and GStreamer builds.** Action: ship an LGPL FFmpeg as separate DLLs/SOs (not the `static` features of `ffmpeg-sys-next`), hardware encoders only, a source offer and build notes on our server, and pick one of FFmpeg or GStreamer.
8. **OpenSeeFace** and **YuNet** have non-commercial training data (WIDER FACE, 300-W, MPIIGaze). Action: do not use their weights; the own-tracker plan already avoids them.

## Unverified (close before shipping)

9. **ICT-FaceKit Light:** no scan-subject consent statement; the registration prior used the Basel Face Model and FaceWarehouse (licences unchecked here; an earlier tracker note calls BFM non-commercial); 99 Triplegangers commercial scans are mixed in; the upstream issue is unanswered. Action: write to USC ICT (README contact) asking for a commercial-use and consent statement for the Light PCA data; check the BFM, FaceWarehouse and Triplegangers terms; in parallel prepare FLAME 2023 Open (CC BY-4.0) plus our own captures as the fallback. Do not train a shipped tracker on ICT renders until answered.
10. **MediaPipe weights:** now Apache-2.0 per the three model cards, but residual risks remain: GHUM licence unverified, a Google APIs ToS layer, telemetry in newer versions with no opt-out. Action: archive the three PDFs with hashes, self-host the WASM and model, pin the web build at 0.10.14, keep Python at 0.10.21 or earlier for anything beyond local dev, and ask legal review about the ToS.
11. **H.264, HEVC and AAC patent licensing** for encoded output. Action: legal review decision (hardware encoders, per-unit royalties, or VP9/AV1-first defaults).
12. **NVIDIA terms:** Audio2Face-3D's Open Model License is revocable and unilaterally updatable, and CUDA, cuDNN and TensorRT redistribution rules come from live-page summaries. Audio2Emotion is gated and unread. Action: legal review read of the exact texts; keep an `ort`-only fallback for lip sync.
