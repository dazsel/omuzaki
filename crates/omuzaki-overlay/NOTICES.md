# Notices for the fonts omuzaki-overlay draws with

The painter (`src/paint.rs`) compiles two faces that other crates already bundle; nothing new is added to the
repository. Both are SIL OFL 1.1 and listed in `docs/licensing/register.md`.

| What | Where it is bundled | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Inter 4.1 SemiBold, Latin subset | `crates/omuzaki-stage/assets/fonts/` (its `NOTICES.md` and `OFL.txt`) | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| Bricolage Grotesque (variable), drawn at weight 800 | `crates/omuzaki-short/assets/fonts/` (its `NOTICES.md` and `OFL-BricolageGrotesque.txt`) | SIL OFL 1.1 |

The fonts are not sold on their own and are not renamed or modified by this crate.
