# Hair follows the head's own size

- Hair now follows `head.size` (#2677): the groups are built and placed against the head at the template's size, then sized with it about the head joint, so a smaller head wears a smaller cut of the same proportions (its volume, clearances, ties and baby hairs too) instead of the template's hair standing off it. At size 1 the hair is unchanged, bit for bit.
- The cap no longer pokes through a curtain at the crown: it stays under the curtain's outer face wherever the curtain lies over it.
- Hair pushed off a relatively thick neck no longer ends up inside the head at the nape.
