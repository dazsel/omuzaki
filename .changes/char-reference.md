# Characters that hold up in a close-up short

- Braids and an ahoge (#2411): a tail can be braided (`braid`: strands, crossings, width, narrowing, depth and the tuft below the band), so a side lock, a back braid or twin braids are a braided tail with spring physics, and its end band rides the braid as it swings. A new `ahoge` generator grows one to three curled strands from the crown, each on its own spring. Tails now keep clear of the body and can fall forward over the shoulder (`forward`).
- Character styles (#2410): `character.set_style` applies a style's face design, face shape, neck and proportions in one undoable step, `character.create` takes a `style`, and `character.list_styles` lists them. `mature` gives an older anime character on the standard and tall templates: narrow almond eyes under heavy, flicked upper lashes, a small mouth and a tick of a nose, the long face shape, a longer neck and broader shoulders, braided locks in front of the shoulders and a two-strand ahoge. A style is a file in `library/styles/`.
