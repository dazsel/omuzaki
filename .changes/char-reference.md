# Characters that hold up in a close-up short

- Braids and an ahoge (#2411): a tail can be braided (`braid`: strands, crossings, width, narrowing, depth and the tuft below the band), so a side lock, a back braid or twin braids are a braided tail with spring physics, and its end band rides the braid as it swings. A new `ahoge` generator grows one to three curled strands from the crown, each on its own spring. Tails now keep clear of the body and can fall forward over the shoulder (`forward`).
- Character styles (#2410): `character.set_style` applies a style's face design, face shape, neck and proportions in one undoable step, `character.create` takes a `style`, and `character.list_styles` lists them. `mature` gives an older anime character on the standard and tall templates: narrow almond eyes under heavy, flicked upper lashes, a small mouth and a tick of a nose, the long face shape, a longer neck and broader shoulders, braided locks in front of the shoulders and a two-strand ahoge. A style is a file in `library/styles/`.
- Close-up hair (#2412): a render at `Offline` quality builds hair for the close-up: twice the ring detail, smoothed lock paths and fine flyaway wisps off the outer locks, so a 1080x1920 face shot shows no facets. The bangs' layers now sit apart at the root instead of cutting through each other, and the crown cap tucks under the locks. Hair takes its rim colour, strength and width from its shading (`rim`, `rim_strength`, `rim_width`), and a `back_light` strength lets the scene's back lights catch its edge.
- Jewellery (#2413): `ring` (on any finger's first segment, either hand), `earrings` (studs, hoops or drops, under each ear or one), `bracelet` (bangle, cuff with a stone, or beads) and `filigree-collar` (twisted gold wire motifs with stones at their feet) part kinds, in two new shading programs any part can use: `metal` (banded highlights, a sky-and-ground reflection, glints) and `gem` (facets, a dark heart, fire and twinkle). Eight pieces of jewellery, the braids, the ahoge and the mature style are in the asset library.
- Gestures that reach (#2414): a pose can say where a hand should *be* (`[reach.right]`: a point on a bone of the same body, in arm lengths, with the elbow's side), and the rig turns it into a hand goal on any body after the pose and before IK, so a hand lands on the chest, two palms meet and an arm stretches to the lens on a small or a tall character. Five gesture clips with their hand shapes (`spread`, `flat`) are in the library: `reach-to-lens`, `hand-on-chest`, `palm-touch`, `point` and `cheer`. `clip.set` authors a character's own clip from keyed poses (library names or inline, mirrored, with hand contacts), `clip.delete` removes it and `clip.list` lists both; `act.clip` plays the character's own clip before the library's. The arm guard now measures the torso as an ellipse (its half width and half depth) and the hand as a flat ellipsoid, so a palm lies on the chest instead of floating a hand's breadth off it, and a kit hand's spread thumb stays joined to the palm.
- Two characters that touch (#2415): `stage.interact` starts a hug, palms together or cheek to cheek between two cast members (or ends one with `off`), and stands the other member for it in the same undoable step. Every frame the cast spaces the pair for their own bodies (chests meeting, an arm's reach, shoulders side by side), gives each one's hands a goal on the other's body (round the back, palm to palm, over the far shoulder and round the waist), turns and leans the heads so they pass or meet, and keeps each one's arms out of the other's body with the arm guard. On the kit's standard and tall characters the chests meet within 5 mm, the cheeks within 15 mm and the palms land on their goals.
- Bodysuit and a close bodice (#2447): a `bodysuit` garment (high, halter, scoop or round neck; straps, short or puff sleeves) whose inside follows the bust and the waist within 4 mm on every template and shape, and the `dress` bodice now follows the body as close.
- Gorget (#2450): the `filigree-collar` takes a `height` up the neck and a `chest` piece down the collarbones, so it becomes a closed gold plate that rises up the neck, rides over the shoulders and lies on the chest as a crescent, with twisted rims, a running scroll (`scroll_scale`, `scroll_depth`) and a large `centre` stone in a bezel, claws or a halo of beads. The neck plate turns with the neck and the chest piece stays on the chest. It never passes through the body on any template or shape. An ornate gorget is in the asset library.
- Hair strands and soft tips (#2451): the hair material reads a generated strand texture (28 strands across each lock with soft dark gaps and a finer grain, drifting gently down the lock) and is cut at its alpha, so every lock's, strip's and wisp's last 12 mm frays into fine points of their own lengths instead of ending in one hard edge. Neighbouring locks show different strands, and the crown cap carries strands radiating from the crown instead of stripes. The texture is generated from integer hashes, the same every build, and exports with the hair.
- One angel ring (#2462): the `hair` program takes the ring's half width, its wander from lock to lock and its comb teeth (`ring-width`, `ring-wobble`, `ring-teeth`), and the kit's hair (`HairShading::ring_width`, `ring_wobble`, `ring_teeth`, settable with `hair.set_shading`) draws a thin, steady arc that runs across the locks and follows the head, where the prototype's band stepped from lock to lock in soft blobs.
- Hair that reads as one mass (#2479, #2481, #2482): the locks' shading normals bend toward the hair's volume (out of the head, and out of a tail's chain along a tail), so the light-to-shade sweep runs across the whole head and the ink outline traces only the hair's silhouette instead of ringing every lock; the inside of a flared lock faces in, so the back light no longer lights it grey; each lock's sides can thin out strand by strand through the alpha cut (a curtain stays one closed sheet); and the strand texture is a system of parameters. `hair.set_shading` takes `normal_blend`, `edge_feather`, `strands`, `strand_gap`, `strand_grain` and `tip_length`.
- Lock shapes for every hair generator (#2480): `hair.group.set_lock` gives a group's locks a cross-section (`lens`, `card` for flat crisp spikes, `round` for heavy locks), a tip (`point`, `fork`, `blunt`, `curl_in`, `curl_out`) and a split into up to four sub-locks that part partway down and fan apart toward their tips. Bangs, framing locks, sweeps, tails and guides all take it; the moved points keep clear of the head and body.
- An angel ring that follows the strands (#2483): the `hair` program's ring can be a highlight along each lock's own direction (`ring-follow`, the kit's default), found from how `hair-uv` runs across the screen, so it bends with every lock and still reads as one band across the crown; `ring-shift` slides it up or down the head, and the prototype's painted ring stays one setting away. Every shading program now gets the screen-space derivatives of its position and first attribute, and the program block holds 48 floats.
- One parting, one whorl, one volume (#2478): a hair style can carry a shared volume and flow (`HairParams::volume`) that every generator reads. `hair.flow.set` sets the parting (how far off the middle, where it starts and ends, or none) and the crown whorl: the scalp shell combs its strands away from the parting and round the whorl, part sweeps root on the parting on their own sides, and the fringe sweeps away from it. `hair.volume.set` sets how far the hair stands off the head (overall, at the crown, at the back), fading out below the ears so hanging hair keeps its fall, or clears the shared volume. A style without one builds exactly as before.
- A library of whole hairstyles (#2486): `hair.style.list` shows ten styles (long straight, bob, twin tails, ponytail, side braid, hime cut, messy short, wavy long, bun and half up), and `hair.style.apply` cuts a hair part in one, replacing its groups and shared parting, whorl and fullness while keeping its colours, shading and physics. Styles are data (`library/hair-styles/<id>.toml`), built only from the generators and layers; adding one is adding a file. The curtain can drape over the head before it falls (`drape_deg`), so a bob or long hair reads as one mass from the crown instead of hanging from under the scalp shell, and framing locks are now kept clear of the head as drawn, as the fringe is (they cut 5 mm into the cheek on the tall template).
- The hair's look (#2516, #2521, #2524, #2525, #2526, #2536): each light now draws two highlights along every lock's strands (`highlight`, `highlight_strength`, `highlight_width`, `highlight_shift`, `highlight_secondary`, `highlight_jitter`, `highlight_cel`), a primary in the light's colour and a wider, hair-tinted secondary that breaks up into glints, from soft to a crisp anime band; the angel ring takes a shape per lock (`ring_shape`: `band`, `zigzag`, `streak` or `crescent`, `ring_shape_depth`); a second, deeper shadow tone (`shade_deep`, `shade_deep_at`) darkens lock undersides and cast shadows; keep-identity keeps the hair's own hue instead of pulling every colour toward cream; and the hair darkens where it is buried in itself and the head (`occlusion`, `occlusion_reach`), baked at build into the vertices' shade so an export carries it. `hair.colour.natural` colours a hair part from melanin and redness, with a dye that shows on light hair and hardly on black, greying and a root-to-tip gradient, fitted to natural hair swatches. `hair.strays.set` sets the flyaways the outer locks now shed at live quality (share, stray, frizz) and baby hairs along the front hairline. `tools/reference-loop/hair_score.py` scores the kit's hair against a VRoid sample's in our renderer at matching framings. Shading programs get 64 floats of parameters.
