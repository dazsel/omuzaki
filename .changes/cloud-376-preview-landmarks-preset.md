# `render.preview` reports the face's landmarks and takes the `measure` render preset

- `render.preview` returns `face_landmarks`: for a character whose painted face is in the picture and turned toward the camera, the pixel positions of its face layout's eye centres, mouth and chin (origin top-left, y down, pixel centres at .5, likeness-measures §5), with `source = "render"`, ready for the `measure.*` ops (#376, design D10). Each point is found on the head's front surface where the base face layer is painted, skinned with the triangle under it, and projected through the shot's camera (`Stage::face_points`). An imported avatar, a face behind the camera or turned away, or an eye outside the picture reports none.
- `render.preview` takes `preset`, a named render preset read from `library/render-presets/<name>.toml` (`recipe::RenderPreset`): the camera, light rig, expression, post effects and line it fixes are applied as data, and the arguments it fixes are refused when given too. `measure` shoots the face framing through an orthographic camera from the front, under `measure-flat`, with the neutral face, no bloom, depth of field, grain or grade, and every line `#1a1a1a` and at least 1.5 px wide at the output size; a picture with fewer than 160 px between the eyes is refused with a hint to give a larger size. An unknown preset is `invalid`, listing the presets.
