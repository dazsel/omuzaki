# Art styles from chibi to photoreal

- Art styles (#2696): `character.set_style` now applies a whole art style in one undoable step: the face design, the face shape, the head and body numbers, the template (a character moves to the style's own when the style isn't tuned for its template, unless `keep_template` asks to refuse), the hairstyle, the look preset and the place on the shading style axis. An `amount` blends from the character as it is (0) to the full style (1), and `character.mix_styles` blends several styles by weight: numbers are the weighted mean, discrete choices (nose kind, template, hairstyle, look) follow the heaviest. Styles own their fields and never compound: a style applied after another is exactly that style on a fresh character. `character.list_styles` groups them by family (anime, western, real) and filters by one.
- Nine new styles beside `mature`: `shoujo`, `shonen`, `retro-cel` (1990s TV cel), `chibi`, `painted`, `animated-feature`, `comic`, `semi-real` and `high-fidelity`, each in the asset library.
- Three new look presets: `cel-flat` (the flat 2D look, LK-06), `semi-real` (halfway to physical with occlusion) and `cinematic` (physical, no lines, depth of field, film grain), each in the asset library.
