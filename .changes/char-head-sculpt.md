# A sculpted head, real ears and skin detail

- Head sculpt (#2697): the head part's new `sculpt` block shapes the face's forms into the head: the brow ridge, the eye sockets, the cheekbones, the temples, the nose (bridge, tip and wings), the muzzle and lips, the chin and the jaw's angle. Each form is placed from the painted face's layout, so it sits under the drawn feature on any template, and all zero (the default) is the smooth anime cranium, so no saved character changes. A sculpted head's mesh is denser at the front, so the nose and lips have the vertices to show. Reachable through `part.set_param` (`sculpt/nose` and so on) and the styles, so through MCP.
- Sculpted ears: a head whose face has an `ear3d` design grows the face's sculpted ear (helix, antihelix, concha, tragus, lobe) out of its own side, through the feature mesher `omuzaki-face` links (`register_feature_mesher!`). A binary that links none builds the plain ear.
- Skin detail: a sculpted head carries `skin-info` for the `skin` program (the cavity closing in the folds, a warm flush on the cheeks and the nose's tip, the curvature for the scattering), so it shades like skin at the physical end of the style axis instead of like a smooth shell.
- The `high-fidelity`, `semi-real`, `animated-feature`, `comic` and `painted` styles sculpt the head, and all but `painted` grow sculpted ears. `high-fidelity` and `semi-real` move the eyes, nose and mouth to adult heights and paint a lighter nose and cheek contour, since the geometry now shades them.
