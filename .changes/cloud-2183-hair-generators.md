# omuzaki-char: every hair generator at prototype parity

- **Curtain (#177, #1679, #1680, #841):** the curtain's strips are meshed and drawn in the hair part (`curtain::group_mesh`: a guide's columns, pointed clumps, the outward bulge), at parity with the prototype's dumps (vertex and triangle counts equal, positions within 2e-7 m). Three fold treatments render for the art director's pick (`Folds::{Prototype, Coherent, Clamped}`); the prototype's stays the default.
- **Bangs (#178, #1684, #1685, #1704, #1890):** the fringe matches the prototype's dumps (width outline within 1e-8 m with #842's smooth profile, tubes within 4e-4 m); the brows show through every preset on every template; `hair.bangs.preset` applies a preset; the clearance sampling is derived from the head the hair sits on.
- **Framing and braid (#176, #1677, #842):** the braid crown and the framing locks at parity (2e-7 m and 3e-7 m); the clump width takes a `TaperShape` (`Linear`, `Smooth`, `Plateau`), `Linear` byte-identical to before; the default framing locks frame the face on all four templates, sized from the head.
- **Tail and bun (#179, #853):** ponytail, twintails, low and high bun presets: broad locks gathered into a tie band (its own material slot), gather locks from the nape into the tie or bun, a spring chain per tail skinned along its locks, and `<group>_tail_root`, `<group>_bun_centre` and `<group>_bun_tie` anchors for accessories.
- **The cap and the checks (#142, #1595, #1655, #1775):** the cap's and part-sweep's remaining style constants are fields with defaults that keep today's output; `HairParams::check` ranges every generator value, each once; the trigger slot range and mask follow `MAX_TRIGGERS`.
- **Verlet (#2120):** four or more overlapping colliders resolve to the nearest clear point; the local parity run against the dumps is 4e-7 m RMS.
- **Budget:** every style on the new `hair-styles` evidence sheet costs 17 000 to 30 000 triangles, under the prototype's own 39 968.
