## omuzaki-char: narrower shoulder hand-over on the arms

- The arm's weights now hand over from the shoulder bone to the upper arm over a narrow band, so a raised arm keeps 84 to 88 % of its volume at 150 degrees (it kept 60 to 75 %).
- Only the arm regions change; the torso, legs, feet, rig and skeleton are as before, and the bind pose is identical.
