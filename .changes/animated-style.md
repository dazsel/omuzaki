# The animated-film look and its motion

- Smear frames: `stage.set_smears` (presets `subtle`, `cartoon` and `wild`, or `amount`, `length_s`, `min_speed` and `jag` by hand) draws a fast move as one streak from where a part was a moment ago, the way animated films draw a swing, while slow moves stay crisp. It works on kit characters and imported avatars. The renderer takes a trailing pose per skinned instance (render-api `Instance::smear`) and pulls the side of each part facing away from its motion back along it, with a ragged edge if asked. Stills draw as posed.
- Squash and stretch: `stage.set_squash` (presets `subtle`, `cartoon` and `rubber`) stretches a body as it rises or falls and squashes it on a landing or push-off, keeping its volume, then bounces it back.
- Animating on twos and threes: `stage.set_timing` holds the cast's poses on twos or threes while the camera, lights and effects keep the frame rate. The face can keep its own beat, and hair and cloth can hold with the body or keep flowing.
- Drawn looks: `stage.set_post {stylize}` adds halftone, hatching, posterize, paper, vignette, lens fringe and split toning. A `stylized` shading program and the `animated-feature`, `cel-anime` and `comic-halftone` look presets are added.
- Staging: cameras take a Dutch-angle `roll`, a `glow` prop is a floating orb of light that lights the scene, and the `swept-up` hairstyle is added.
