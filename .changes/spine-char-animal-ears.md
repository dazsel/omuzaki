# Animal ears

- Animal ears (#162): set the head's ears to `animal` and pick a species (cat, fox, rabbit or wolf) for ears that stand on top of the head, rising through the hair: a cupped fur shell in the hair's colour with a soft inner panel, flush with the head and the pair mirror-symmetric. Rabbit ears sway on a short spring chain. Each ear publishes an `ear_top_l`/`ear_top_r` anchor at its tip for hats and accessories, and recolouring the hair recolours the fur.
