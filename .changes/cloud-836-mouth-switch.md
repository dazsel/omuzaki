# omuzaki-face: the mouth's closed-to-open switch

- The mouth no longer jumps in weight when the jaw opens past an opening of 0.06: the closed line thins by a fifth
  towards the switch, the rim round the open mouth starts as that line, and over the openings 0.06 to 0.2 it becomes
  the open mouth's two-tone rim while the lens deepens from 70% to all of its depth.
- A closed smile's fang fades out over the same range as the open fangs take over, and the lower-lip gloss follows the
  lens down instead of dropping at the switch.
- Mouths open 0.2 or more, and closed mouths, are drawn as before.
