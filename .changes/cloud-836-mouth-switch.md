# omuzaki-face: the mouth's closed-to-open switch

- The mouth no longer jumps in weight when the jaw opens past an opening of 0.06, smiling or not: the closed line thins
  by a fifth towards the switch, the rim round the open mouth starts as that line, and over the openings 0.06 to 0.2 it
  becomes the open mouth's two-tone rim.
- Over the same range the open lens deepens from 0.6 of the closed line's thickness (at most its own depth, whatever
  the profile's line width and scale) to its full depth, and its corners move from the closed line's curve to the open
  shape's.
- A closed smile's fang fades out over the same range as the open fangs take over, and the lower-lip gloss follows the
  lens down instead of dropping at the switch.
- Mouths open 0.2 or more, and closed mouths, are drawn as before.
